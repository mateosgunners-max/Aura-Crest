Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our advisory engagements, timelines, pricing structures, and confidentiality.

Our initial diagnostic sprints take exactly 30 days, yielding actionable immediate improvements. Most enterprise clients engage us on a 6 to 12-month rolling partnership to execute multi-phase transformations such as ERP migration, margin restructuring, or M&A exit preparation. We do not lock clients into rigid multi-year agreements; retainers operate on mutual 30-day notice after the initial 90-day milestone.
While our financial and operational frameworks apply universally, our primary client concentration is in B2B Professional Services, Logistics & Supply Chain, Healthcare & Life Sciences, Industrial Manufacturing, and Commercial Software/SaaS. We specifically avoid pre-revenue consumer startups to maintain our high focus on cash-flow optimization and enterprise EBITDA expansion.
We believe in total fee transparency. Rather than opaque hourly billing that incentivizes inefficiency, we work strictly on fixed monthly retainers ($1,490 – $6,950/mo) or predetermined fixed-fee milestone projects. For select private equity readiness or M&A transactions, we may incorporate an agreed performance success fee tied directly to enterprise valuation achieved.
Prior to commencing any engagement, we establish baseline financial KPIs: Gross Margin, Net Operating Margin, Days Sales Outstanding (DSO), Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), and Monthly Overhead. We benchmark these quarterly. Over 92% of our clients recoup their entire annual advisory fee within the first 120 days via recaptured revenue leakage or overhead reduction.
The Scale Growth Partner ($3,490/mo) tier is tailored for established companies seeking weekly operational and financial oversight to scale from $2M to $10M in annual revenue. The Executive Enterprise ($6,950/mo) tier includes an embedded multidisciplinary team, comprehensive sell-side M&A data room preparation, custom engineering of proprietary digital pipelines, and direct institutional investor introductions.

Confidentiality is the bedrock of our practice. Prior to receiving any internal data, we execute a mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) with comprehensive non-solicitation and data security provisions. All client documentation is stored within SOC-2 Type II certified, encrypted virtual repositories with strict role-based access restricted solely to assigned senior partners.
Onboarding is streamlined into three discrete steps: (1) Initial 45-minute confidential discovery consultation; (2) Execution of NDA, engagement scope, and access to secure client portal; (3) 90-minute Executive Kickoff session within 5 business days of agreement signing to initiate your 30-Day Diagnostic Sprint.
